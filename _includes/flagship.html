Bloodstream

Solo: Started as a 2-day jam (~12 hours)

A 3D microscopic strategy game set inside blood vessels between immune cells and pathogens. Players spend DNA to spawn bacteria and white cells against the opposing faction, buy permanent upgrades, and switch between a top-down command view and an interior camera. I modelled the custom assets in Maya and built the game in Unity URP.

Crown of Domination

Solo: January to April 2026

A social multiplayer RPG built on feudal power dynamics, where players vote, conspire, and overthrow each other to claim the crown. Two factions of up to four players, built in Unity 6 on Photon Fusion 2. Clients predict movement and attacks locally, while the host stays the only authority on damage, roles, and crown ownership. Not publicly playable; builds are available on request.
