I'm Furkan Canturk, a Unity gameplay systems designer and programmer with 2+ years of experience in C# and rapid prototyping. I previously worked as a junior developer intern at Phantom Compass.

I have shipped 14+ games and projects across game jams, simulations, and commercial releases. They include two Unity Asset Store packages, Block Breakout on Google Play, and ORDEAL, a gamified fitness system sold on Amazon and Gumroad. I'm now working toward a Steam release of Bloodstream, a microscopic strategy game set inside blood vessels between immune cells and pathogens.

Based in Kitchener, Ontario. Open to gameplay programming and design roles.
