Personal Game / Simulation Projects

Tape of Life

Itch.io

Solo: ~3 weeks (40+ hours)

A 2D real-time simulation of evolution by mutation and natural selection. It starts with LUCA, the last universal common ancestor, near volcanic vents on the ocean floor about 4 billion years ago. You adjust temperature, pH, oxygen, light, toxins, and radiation, and depending on the conditions, LUCA can diverge into Bacteria, Archaea, and, rarely, Eukarya.

Time Dilation
Simulation

Itch.io

Solo: ~10 days (20 hours)

A 3D simulation of special and general relativity built in Unity. You fly a ship to stars, planets, and black holes, and compare your ship's clock with Earth's. The faster you go or the deeper you get into strong gravity, the slower your clock ticks. At 99.9% of light speed, it runs about 22 times slower than Earth's.

Encyclopedia
Galactica

Itch.io

Solo: ~10 hours

An interactive Drake Equation simulator inspired by Carl Sagan's Cosmos. Players adjust the variables to estimate how many civilizations exist across the Milky Way, then browse a procedural catalogue of alien entries. I built it in Unity with 3D space visuals.

Game Design Devlog

Block Breakout

Itch.io Google Play

Solo: Shipped to Google Play

An arcade paddle game built in Unity and my first release to the Google Play Store. Scoring, difficulty curves, and power-up values are driven by CSV data read at runtime, so tuning never required a rebuild. Getting it live meant Android build settings, keystore signing, and working through internal testing, open testing, and production under Play Console policy.

Realms of Uldar
The Void Crystal

Itch.io

Solo: 5 days (~27 hours), 1-week game jam

A fast-paced top-down action RPG built in 5 days for a game jam. You play the Void Wraith, unlocking and upgrading 7 abilities across 15 levels while racing to destroy the Void Crystal before it charges. I drew all sprites in Photoshop and built the 2D URP lighting from scratch.

Post Mortem

Starcruiser

Itch.io

Solo: 4 days (35 hours), GMTK Game Jam 2026

A 3D FPS and strategy hybrid built in 35 hours for GMTK Game Jam 2026. Players fly with a jetpack and switch between a single-shot rifle, an automatic rifle, and an AoE blaster. They build and protect Rigs to collect energy while defending the Nexus against orcs and alien soldiers.

Sled Slope

Itch.io

Team of 4: Game jam

An endless platformer made in Unity for a game jam with a team of four. You steer a penguin on a sled, dodge obstacles, collect power-ups, and try to reach as many houses as you can. I programmed and designed the player and power-up abilities, and drew the 2D art for the obstacles and some of the houses.
